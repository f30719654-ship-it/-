# Bed Aura Client (simple)

Client-side Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.11: places cobblestone between you and target, places a bed where you look, and activates it (works in The End). Default key: V.

Usage:
1. Build with `./gradlew build`.
2. Put the produced jar into your mods folder for Fabric 1.21.11.
3. Ensure you are in The End and have cobblestone and a bed in your hotbar.
4. Look at a block where you want the bed foot to be, press V.

Warning: This is a client-side helper and may not perfectly protect you on servers with anti-cheat.
